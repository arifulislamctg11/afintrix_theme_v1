Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This platform is operated by Afintrix Advisory Analytics for the management of accounting, inventory, people and reporting data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how it is protected.

Information we hold

The platform stores the business records you and your team enter — such as customers, suppliers, invoices, stock and employee records — together with the account details used to sign in (name, email and role).

How it is used

  • To operate the ERP features you use day to day.
  • To authenticate users and enforce access permissions.
  • To produce the reports and dashboards you request.

Access & security

Access is limited to authorised users of your organisation. Traffic to the platform is served over HTTPS, and data is held on the organisation's own hosted instance. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

Your rights

You may request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of the personal data held about you, subject to the records the business is required to retain. To make a request, contact your administrator or reach us at privacy@afintrix.com.
