Help Center

We're here to get you moving.

Welcome to the Afintrix ERP help center. Whether you're signing in for the first time or need a hand with a specific module, start here.

Signing in

Use the work email and password provided by your administrator. Forgot it? Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page.

Getting access

Need an account or a permission changed? Your organisation's Afintrix administrator can set that up for you.

Email support

Reach the team at support@afintrix.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in page choose “Forgot password?” and follow the emailed link, or ask your administrator to reset it.

Which modules are available?
Accounting, inventory, people and reporting are all included — you'll see them in the sidebar once you sign in, based on your role.

Something isn't working.
Email support@afintrix.com with a short description and a screenshot if you can, and we'll take a look.
